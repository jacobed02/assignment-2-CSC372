About UNC Lexington

The University of North Carolina at Lexington is committed to creating a strong, active, and welcoming campus community. We host events that help students connect, learn, and celebrate campus life.

From academic programs to arts and culture, our events give students opportunities to grow, meet people, and be part of something meaningful.
