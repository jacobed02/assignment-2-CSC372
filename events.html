Featured Event

Spring Concert on the Quad

Category: Music

Date: April 12, 2026

Time: 7:00 PM

Location: University Quad

Celebrate the start of spring with music, food trucks, and a campus-wide gathering designed for students and families alike.

About this event

The Spring Concert on the Quad brings together student performers, guest musicians, and the wider UNC Lexington community for a night of energy and celebration. This outdoor event is a welcoming space for students to relax, connect, and enjoy live entertainment in the heart of campus.

What to expect

  • Live performances by student bands and guest artists
  • Food trucks and campus vendor booths
  • Seating areas, open lawn space, and photo-friendly event zones

Schedule

  • 5:30 PM — Gates open and food vendors begin service
  • 6:30 PM — Opening performance and welcome remarks
  • 7:00 PM — Main concert set begins
  • 8:45 PM — Closing performance and campus spotlight

Accessibility & participation

The event is designed to be inclusive and accessible. Seating, shaded areas, and accessible routes will be available throughout the quad. Guests are welcome to bring blankets or small chairs, and all students are encouraged to attend.

Event Details

  • Date: April 12, 2026
  • Time: 7:00 PM
  • Location: University Quad
  • Organizer: Campus Activities Board
  • Admission: Free for students, $10 for guests
